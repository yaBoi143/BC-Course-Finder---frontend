Career Overview

A Data Scientist analyses complex digital information to find patterns, predict trends, and support organisational decision-making. This role combines statistical analysis, computer programming, and domain expertise to transform raw data into actionable business intelligence.

Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a Data Scientist and a Data Analyst?
Data Analysts examine historical data to describe past performance. Data Scientists use predictive modelling and machine learning to forecast future outcomes, requiring deeper programming and statistical expertise.
Is advanced mathematics necessary?
Statistical literacy is essential, but advanced calculus is handled through computational tools. Logical reasoning and problem-solving are more critical than manual computation.
How does AI impact data science careers?
AI and AutoML tools enhance productivity but don't replace human judgment in problem formulation, data quality assessment, ethics review and interpretation. The field continues evolving toward higher-level analytical strategy.
Are SA qualifications recognised internationally?
South African degrees from accredited universities are well-regarded, particularly within Commonwealth countries. International mobility may require credential evaluation depending on the destination country.
