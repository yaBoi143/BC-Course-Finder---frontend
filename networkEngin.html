Network Engineer

Design and maintain the communication networks that connect devices, systems and people around the world.

Diploma in IT · NQF 6 Infrastructure Stream

Career Overview

A Network Engineer designs, builds, and maintains computer networks that allow devices and systems to communicate with each other. These networks can include office networks, internet systems, and cloud-based infrastructure. Network engineers ensure that data flows efficiently and securely between computers, making them essential in almost every modern organisation.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Setting up and configuring computer networks and network hardware
  • Monitoring network performance and fixing connectivity issues
  • Maintaining routers, switches, and servers
  • Ensuring network security and preventing unauthorised access
  • Troubleshooting network problems and providing technical support
