Diploma vs Degree

Not sure which qualification is right for you? Here's everything you need to make an informed decision.

Which path is right for you?

Belgium Campus offers both a Diploma and a Degree in Information Technology. Both are respected qualifications — the right choice depends on your goals, timeline and preferred learning style.

📋

Diploma in IT

3-Year Qualification · NQF Level 6

  • ✅ Shorter duration — enter the workforce sooner
  • ✅ Highly practical and hands-on
  • ✅ Specialise in Infrastructure or Software Development
  • ✅ Lower total study cost
  • ✅ Ideal if you want to start working quickly
  • ✅ Can upgrade to a degree later
  • ⚠️ Fewer senior management roles without further study
  • ⚠️ Some employers prefer a degree for certain positions
VS
🎓

Bachelor's Degree in IT

4-Year Qualification · NQF Level 7

  • ✅ Broader, deeper academic foundation
  • ✅ Opens doors to senior and specialised roles
  • ✅ Recognised internationally
  • ✅ Can pursue Honours and postgraduate study
  • ✅ Specialise in Data Science or Software Engineering
  • ✅ Higher earning potential over career
  • ⚠️ One additional year of study
  • ⚠️ Higher total cost
