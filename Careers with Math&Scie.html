Careers with Maths & Physical Science

Your strong foundation in Maths and Physical Science opens doors to some of the most in-demand IT careers.

Why Maths & Science?

Logical thinking, problem solving and an understanding of how the physical world works are at the core of many IT disciplines. If you excelled at Maths or Physical Science at school, you already have the mindset needed to thrive in these fields.

💻

Software Engineer

Bachelor of Computing

Design and build software systems using mathematical algorithms, data structures and engineering principles.

  • Strong Maths required (algorithms, discrete maths)
  • Physical Science builds logical reasoning
  • Avg salary: R450 000 – R900 000/year
📊

Data Scientist

Bachelor of Information Technology

Use statistics, calculus and machine learning to extract insights from large datasets and build predictive models.

  • Advanced Maths essential (statistics, linear algebra)
  • Physical Science underpins modelling concepts
  • Avg salary: R500 000 – R1 000 000/year
☁️

Cloud & Systems Architect

Bachelor of Computing

Architect scalable cloud infrastructure and distributed systems, requiring deep understanding of networks and computing theory.

  • Maths used in performance modelling
  • Physics of electricity aids hardware understanding
  • Avg salary: R600 000 – R1 200 000/year
🔐

CyberSecurity Analyst

Bachelor of Information Technology

Apply cryptography, number theory and logical analysis to protect systems and networks from threats.

  • Cryptography is built on advanced Maths
  • Analytical thinking from Science is key
  • Avg salary: R480 000 – R950 000/year
🤖

AI / ML Engineer

Bachelor of Computing

Build intelligent systems using calculus, probability and linear algebra to train machine learning models.

  • Calculus and linear algebra are foundational
  • Physical Science reasoning transfers directly
  • Avg salary: R700 000 – R1 500 000/year
🌐

Network Engineer

Diploma in Information Technology

Design and maintain network infrastructure using principles of signal transmission and electromagnetic theory.

  • Physical Science (electricity, waves) is directly relevant
  • Maths used in subnetting and routing
  • Avg salary: R350 000 – R750 000/year
