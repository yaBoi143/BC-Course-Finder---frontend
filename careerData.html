Data Scientist

Turn raw data into powerful insights that shape business decisions and drive innovation across industries.

Bachelor of Computing · NQF 8 Data Science Stream

Career Overview

A Data Scientist collects, analyses and interprets large amounts of data to help organisations make informed decisions. They use mathematics, statistics, programming and machine learning techniques to discover patterns, predict future trends and solve complex business problems. Data Scientists are increasingly important in industries that rely on data-driven decision-making.

Typical Responsibilities

  • Collecting and cleaning large datasets
  • Analysing data to identify patterns and trends
  • Building predictive models using machine learning
  • Creating dashboards and data visualisations
  • Presenting findings to business stakeholders
  • Supporting strategic business decisions with data insights
